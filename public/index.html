V · ORDERS OF THE DAY

Straight answers.

What happens when my term ends?+

Monitoring stops. Your data stays available to export for 30 days, then it's deleted. Nothing renews unless you buy another term.

What counts as a monitored service?+

Any single endpoint, host, database or storage bucket Castra checks. A load balancer with five instances behind it counts as one.

Can I upgrade partway through?+

Yes. You pay the difference, prorated by the days you have left, and the new term starts the day you upgrade.

Is there a free trial?+

The 30-day Contubernium is the trial, and it's refundable in full within the first 14 days if Castra isn't for you.

Can we pay by invoice or purchase order?+

Yes, on every term. Bank transfer, 30-day invoice terms and POs are all fine. Card works too if you'd rather be done in a minute.

Do you charge per seat?+

No. Invite the whole team. Price is set by term length and the size of your formation, not headcount.
