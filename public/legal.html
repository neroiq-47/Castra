Legal.

The short version of how Castra handles terms, your data and cookies. Full contract documents are issued with every order.

Terms

Each Castra term is a single, one-time purchase for a fixed number of days. It starts on the day payment is confirmed and ends on the stated end date. It never renews automatically. The full terms of service are sent with your order confirmation and are available before purchase on request through our contact form.

Privacy

When you send a form on this site we collect what you type into it: your name, work email, company and any message or order details. We use it only to reply to you and, if you buy, to set up your term. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

  • Form submissions are stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, on our behalf.
  • Card payments are processed by Stripe. Castra never sees or stores card details.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time through the contact form.

Data processing agreement

Castra signs a data processing agreement under UK and EU GDPR with any customer who asks. Request a copy, together with the security pack, through the contact form.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If you start a card checkout on Stripe's payment page, Stripe may set cookies needed to process the payment and prevent fraud.
